# Slice

## Intent

I want depth slices I can paint, add, remove, duplicate, and turn, in a spacious slice view with the tools where I need them.

## Criteria

- **SLICE-2**  I can paint and erase cells by dragging on the depth slice I have selected.
- **SLICE-3**  I can add, remove, and duplicate a depth slice, and turn the selected slice a quarter turn clockwise.
- **SLICE-18**  The sculpture is the focus of the window, and I can switch to a spacious slice-editing view with tools shown where I need them.
