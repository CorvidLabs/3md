# Examples

## Intent

I want to browse the Examples directory on the axes Examples/README.md already names, and I want to open that text catalog in the hosted gallery.

I want to open the canopy and shared-grove files in Examples/Extensions as text, kind 1, and kind 2, and I want to open gdscript/examples/layers.3md and see its planes as data.

## Criteria

- **EXAMPLES-1**  I can browse the Examples directory across the 21 axes Examples/README.md names: time, frame, step, depth, layer, space, floor, slide, status, version, section, stage, measure, mileage, panel, period, round, row, scene, shot, and stanza.
- **EXAMPLES-2**  I can open the text catalog in the hosted gallery.
- **EXAMPLES-3**  I can open canopy and shared grove from Examples/Extensions as text in canopy.3md and shared-grove.3md, as uncompressed kind 1 in canopy.3mdb and shared-grove.3mdb, as LZFSE kind 1 in canopy.lzfse.3mdb and shared-grove.lzfse.3mdb, and as kind 2 in canopy.structured.3mdb and shared-grove.structured.3mdb.
- **EXAMPLES-4**  I can open the Godot sample gdscript/examples/layers.3md and see its planes as data.
- **EXAMPLES-5**  I can treat each example as a document. A binary twin or a game scene is present only where that file exists.
